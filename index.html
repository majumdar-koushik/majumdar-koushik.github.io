The problem

When everything is visible, everything competes for attention.

Every day brings new ideas, new responsibilities and new urgent work. The list keeps growing. Eventually it can become so long that it stops helping.

For years, the search was for a better task list. The turning point was realizing that long-term recurring commitments could quietly disappear beneath everyday urgency.

How do I keep my long-term commitments alive while dealing with today’s demands?

The approach

You don't lose the rest of your commitments. You simply stop carrying all of them in your head at once.

01

HIDE

Choose how many Today’s tasks you want to see. Drag the ones you need into the visible window. Future and less-urgent work stays out of the way.

When a visible task is completed, the next task in the queue automatically appears.

02

ROLLOVER

Recurring commitments return when they are due. Daily and weekly recurring tasks appear in Today’s list on their due dates; monthly tasks remain in their monthly cycle.

03

STREAKS

Every recurring completion builds a visible history. Streaks show continuity, while accumulated misses remain visible in the calendar.
