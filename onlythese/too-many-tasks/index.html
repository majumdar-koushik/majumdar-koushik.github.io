Too Many Tasks? Focus on What Needs Attention Now

When a task list contains work, personal commitments, recurring activities and future plans, the difficulty is deciding which ones deserve your attention now.

A simpler way to handle a long task list

You do not need to treat every task as equally immediate. Keep your commitments in the task manager, but separate the small set that needs attention today from work that can wait.

The aim is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

What to do

  • Limit the number of Today tasks you see.
  • Put the tasks you need into the visible order.
  • Complete the task in front of you instead of repeatedly scanning the whole list.
  • Keep missed, future and completed work available without making it compete with Today.

How OnlyThese applies this idea

This is the problem OnlyThese is designed to address. Its Today view lets you choose how many tasks you want visible at a time. When a visible task is completed, the next task in the queue can appear.
