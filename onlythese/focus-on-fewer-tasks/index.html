OnlyThese · Choose a smaller working set

Focus on a Small Number of Tasks at a Time

A long list may be necessary, but you do not necessarily need to work from the whole list at once.

Choose a smaller working set

Set a practical number of tasks for immediate attention and work through that set. The rest of your commitments can remain available without competing with the work in front of you.

The aim is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

What to do

  • Choose how many Today tasks you want displayed.
  • Drag to reorder the visible tasks when your focus changes.
  • Complete a visible task and continue to the next.
  • Keep other commitments accessible outside the immediate view.

How OnlyThese applies this idea

OnlyThese makes the size of the Today view a user preference. Its task queue can bring the next task into the visible window as you complete the current one.
