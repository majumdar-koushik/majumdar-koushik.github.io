A SMALLER WORKING SET

Focus on a Small Number of Tasks at a Time

A smaller working set can make a long list easier to act on.

Why fewer visible tasks can help

When many tasks are visible, each one can compete for attention. A smaller working set gives you a clearer immediate view while the rest of your commitments remain recorded elsewhere.

Choose your own limit

OnlyThese does not force one universal number of tasks. You choose how many Today tasks you want displayed and can reorder them according to what you need to focus on.

The goal is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

Let the queue move forward

When you complete a visible task, another task can enter the visible window. This creates a simple progression: work on the tasks in front of you, complete them, and continue.

A simpler way to work from your task list

  • Set the number of Today tasks you want displayed.
  • Drag tasks to reorder the visible set.
  • Complete one and allow the next task into view.
  • Keep future, missed and completed work accessible.
