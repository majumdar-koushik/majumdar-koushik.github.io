WHAT TO DO FIRST

How to Decide What to Do First

The first decision becomes easier when fewer tasks are competing for it.

Why the first decision can feel overwhelming

If your task manager opens to dozens of tasks, deciding what to do first requires scanning the whole list. The list becomes part of the problem rather than simply a record of your commitments.

Make the first decision smaller

You can make the decision easier by reducing the number of tasks that need immediate consideration. This does not mean abandoning the rest. It means separating immediate attention from everything that can wait.

The goal is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

OnlyThese starts with a focused Today view

A simpler way to work from your task list

  • Reduce the number of tasks competing for attention.
  • Choose and reorder the Today tasks you want visible.
  • Complete the task in front of you.
  • Keep other commitments accessible for later.
