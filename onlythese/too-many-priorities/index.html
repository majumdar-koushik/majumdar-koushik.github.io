Too Many Priorities? Focus on What Matters Now

Many tasks can genuinely matter. The difficulty is trying to give all of them your attention at the same time.

Prioritize the work in front of you, not everything at once

Instead of repeatedly ranking your entire task list, decide which small set needs attention now. Keep the remaining commitments available without making them part of the immediate decision.

The aim is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

What to do

  • Choose the number of Today tasks you can focus on.
  • Order those tasks according to your current priorities.
  • Complete them one at a time.
  • Keep future and recurring commitments available for later.

How OnlyThese applies this idea

OnlyThese lets you choose the size of the Today view and drag tasks to reorder it. The product is designed around managing attention rather than requiring the whole task list to be prioritized at once.
