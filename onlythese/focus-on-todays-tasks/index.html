Focus on Today's Tasks Without Losing Future Tasks

Focusing on today does not mean forgetting tomorrow. The key is to separate what you need to act on now from what you need to remember for later.

Give today's work a focused view

Keep future commitments in your task manager, but do not make them compete with today's work. Work from a limited Today view and return to future tasks when they become due.

The aim is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

What to do

  • Show only the number of Today tasks you can comfortably work with.
  • Keep future commitments stored and accessible.
  • Let recurring tasks return according to their recurrence.
  • Use missed and completed sections separately from today's working view.

How OnlyThese applies this idea

OnlyThese follows this model. Its Today view is focused, while future, missed and completed work remains accessible. Recurring tasks return when they are due.
