ENDLESS TASK LISTS

An Endless Task List Does Not Have to Be Your Daily View

Your task list can be long without your daily working view having to be.

A long list creates a visibility problem

The longer a task list becomes, the more often you have to scan, filter and reconsider it. A list can successfully store commitments and still be a poor place to decide what deserves attention right now.

Keep the list. Change what you look at

Instead of trying to make the entire list equally manageable, you can keep the full set of commitments while limiting the number of tasks that compete for immediate attention.

The goal is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

OnlyThese uses a limited attention window

In OnlyThese, you choose how many Today tasks you want to display. You can reorder them, complete them, and allow the next task in the queue to appear. Other task sections remain available when you need them.

A simpler way to work from your task list

  • Keep long-term commitments without displaying them all.
  • Choose a smaller Today window.
  • Reorder tasks when your focus changes.
  • Let completed work make room for the next task.
