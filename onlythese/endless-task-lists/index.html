An Endless Task List Does Not Have to Be Your Daily View

A long task list can be useful as a record of commitments but overwhelming as a working screen.

Keep the list, reduce the immediate view

You do not have to shorten your entire task list just to make today manageable. Instead, reduce the number of tasks that compete for your attention at one time.

The aim is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

What to do

  • Keep long-term and future commitments recorded.
  • Choose a limited number of Today tasks.
  • Reorder the tasks you want to work on now.
  • Let another task enter the working view as you complete one.

How OnlyThese applies this idea

OnlyThese uses a user-controlled Today task count and a task queue. This lets the complete set of commitments remain available without forcing all of them into the immediate view.
