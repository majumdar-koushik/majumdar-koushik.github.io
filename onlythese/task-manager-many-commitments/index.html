MANY COMMITMENTS

A Task Manager for People With Lots of Ongoing Commitments

When your responsibilities span work, personal life, projects and recurring activities, a simple list can become difficult to manage.

One list can become too much to look at

People with several interests or ongoing commitments may have a task list that grows faster than it can be reviewed. The challenge is not necessarily having commitments; it is having all of them competing for attention at once.

Keep the commitments. Reduce the competition

A useful task manager should let you retain long-term commitments without requiring you to look at all of them whenever you open the app. Immediate work and future commitments can have different places in the task cycle.

The goal is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

OnlyThese is built around that separation

OnlyThese has Today, Missed and future task areas. Daily, weekly and monthly recurring commitments continue according to their recurrence. The Today view can be limited to the number of tasks you choose.

A simpler way to work from your task list

  • Manage daily, weekly and monthly recurring tasks.
  • Keep future commitments in the system.
  • Choose the number of Today tasks you see.
  • Use task records, streaks and data-management features when needed.
