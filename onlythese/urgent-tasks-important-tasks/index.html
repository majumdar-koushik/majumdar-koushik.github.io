URGENT VS IMPORTANT

When Urgent Tasks Bury Important Work

Urgent work can take over your attention while important commitments quietly disappear from view.

The problem is not only urgency

Some tasks need action immediately. Others matter because they are recurring, long-term or easy to forget. If your task manager puts everything into one constantly visible list, urgent work can dominate simply because it is in front of you.

Keep important commitments without keeping everything visible

Important work should not have to compete with every immediate demand. The goal is to keep commitments alive while allowing today's work to receive focused attention.

The goal is not to manage less. It is to decide what deserves your attention now.

How OnlyThese handles the attention problem

OnlyThese keeps future and recurring commitments in the system while focusing the Today view on a limited number of tasks. Missed work can return to view when it needs attention, and recurring commitments return when they are due.

A simpler way to work from your task list

  • Focus the Today view on immediate work.
  • Keep recurring commitments alive until they are due.
  • Bring missed work back into view.
  • Avoid deleting important tasks simply because something more urgent appeared.
